Technical Challenges

I faced 3 main difficulties during this project:

  • Shipping Logic: Writing code to calculate different shipping dates and prices for each order.
  • Search Issues: Making the search bar find the right products quickly and correctly.
  • Data Management: Keeping track of customer orders so they don't get lost when the page reloads.

03
How I Solved Them

  • For Shipping: I used a "Date Library" to automate delivery time calculations.
  • For Search: I used "Array Filtering" to match user text with our product list.
  • For Data: I used LocalStorage to save the order information safely.
